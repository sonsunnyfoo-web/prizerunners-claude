Day by day
  • Visits: one per browser per page, counted live. Test visits with ?lp= in the link are left out.
  • Clicks: visitors who tapped a sign-up button.
  • Regs and FTDs: real registrations from the registrations sheet, matched by the page label (lp_a / lp_g). They update when the sheet updates, so the latest day fills in later.
  • Reg rate = regs ÷ visits. FTD rate = FTDs ÷ regs.
