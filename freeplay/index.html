FAQs

Got Questions?

How are winners selected?

Winners are determined by which submitted coordinate is closest to the actual impact point. Tie-breakers are handled according to the game rules or Terms and Conditions.

When will the winners be announced?

Winners are usually announced within 24 hours after the competition closes. Specific timelines will be posted on the competition page.

How do I claim my prize if I win?

If you win, PrizeRunners will contact you via the on-site messaging system, email, or phone. Make sure your account contact details are accurate.

How will I know if I've won?

Winners are notified directly, and results are published on the platform after the competition closes so everyone can verify them.

How many lines can I draw?

There's no limit. You can draw as many lines as needed to help judge the impact point, but keeping your screen clear is recommended.

Can I change my submission after I've entered?

No. Submissions are final once confirmed, so double-check your chosen coordinates before submitting.

Can I enter multiple times?

Yes, you can enter as many times as you like. Each entry requires a separate ticket and submission, and players should play responsibly.

How can I buy a ticket to play?

PrizeRunners supports credit and debit cards, Google Pay, Apple Pay, and the option to add the ticket cost to your phone bill.
